I am a software test engineer specializing in automation, frequently crossing into development and operations. I am interested in both **how systems work and how they are built** — from planning, design, and implementation through testing, deployment, and operations to monitoring and optimization (the entire SDLC).

Starting in web and mobile application testing, I later moved on to complex projects in **big data, blockchain, and cloud**. Most recently, I have developed an interest in **DevOps and infrastructure**.

I am happy to share my knowledge, experiences, and insights on these and other topics through this blog.
